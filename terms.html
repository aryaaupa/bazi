A working
demonstration.

Bazi’s public website and workspace are a synthetic product demonstration. Use generated records only. The synthetic provider role, approvals, follow-ups, and integration examples do not establish a clinical relationship or perform real patient outreach.

Research boundary

The frozen reference model has synthetic provenance and has not been clinically validated. Its scores are engagement signals, not calibrated clinical probabilities. The workspace does not diagnose, prescribe, modify treatment, or replace a clinician. Simulated before-and-after observations do not establish intervention effectiveness.

Pilot drafts and integrations

Generated protocols are drafts for discussion, not approved studies. FHIR exports are mapping illustrations, not working EHR integrations. External deployment and real data require appropriate agreements, institutional reviews, integration testing, and independently evaluated safeguards.
