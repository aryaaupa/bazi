QUESTIONS

What a care team should know.

What does Bazi help a care team see?+

Bazi brings changes in participation, session duration, timing, and reported barriers into one longitudinal view. Its reference signal helps frame a provider review; it does not diagnose or modify treatment.

What can I try in the workspace?+

Explore Aadi’s synthetic record, advance the observed timeline, inspect the evidence, record a provider decision, compare simulated follow-ups, and explore the benchmark and governance views.

Does Bazi automatically contact patients?+

No. This research workspace sends no outreach. Permitted engagement actions require a recorded provider approval.

Has the model been clinically validated?+

No. The frozen reference model has synthetic provenance and has not been clinically validated. Its scores are research signals, not calibrated clinical probabilities.

What would a first collaboration look like?+

Start with a jointly defined engagement endpoint and a governed retrospective dataset. Evaluate historical records first, without patient-facing decisions.
