A few things to know.

What can I try in the demo?+

Advance Maya’s synthetic history, inspect the changing signal, review an engagement action, and record a simulated follow-up. You can also explore the cohort, examine validation reports, export records, and draft a pilot protocol.

Does Bazi automatically contact patients?+

Every permitted action in the demo needs provider approval. This synthetic workspace sends no patient outreach. Decisions and follow-ups remain in your browser.

Is the model clinically validated?+

The frozen reference model has synthetic provenance. This demo exercises the software and workflow; it does not establish clinical accuracy or benefit. Its scores are engagement signals, not calibrated clinical probabilities.

Can I use real patient data here?+

Use generated records only. A real-data deployment needs a separate environment with reviewed authentication, consent, privacy, security, retention, and institutional agreements.
