A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What does Bazi help a care team see?+

Bazi brings changes in participation, session duration, timing, and reported barriers into one longitudinal view. Its reference signal helps frame a provider review; it does not diagnose or modify treatment.

What can I try in the workspace?+

Explore Maya’s synthetic record, advance the observed timeline, inspect the evidence, record a provider decision, and compare simulated follow-ups. You can also explore validation, export records, and generate a draft study protocol.

Does Bazi automatically contact patients?+

No outreach is sent from this research workspace. Permitted engagement actions require a recorded provider approval. All decisions and synthetic follow-ups remain in your browser.

Has the model been clinically validated?+

The frozen reference model has synthetic provenance and has not been clinically validated. Its scores are research signals, not calibrated clinical probabilities. Generated outcomes do not establish clinical benefit.

What would a first collaboration look like?+

Start with a jointly defined engagement endpoint and a governed retrospective dataset. The proposed first phase evaluates historical records without patient-facing decisions. Real data requires a separate environment and appropriate agreements and review.
