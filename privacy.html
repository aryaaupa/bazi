DEMO DATA HANDLING · OCTOBER 4, 2026

Privacy in the synthetic workspace

This website demonstrates a research product using generated participants and events. Do not enter or import information about real patients.

What the demo stores

The cohort is regenerated from a deterministic seed. Added synthetic patients, events, review decisions, audit records, and pilot drafts are saved in this browser’s local storage. They are not synchronized between devices. Demo operations do not send patient records to an external backend. The hosting provider may process ordinary request metadata for delivery and security.

Your controls

Export the current synthetic state in Governance. Reset the workspace to remove its saved demo records, or clear site data in your browser. A service worker caches public website files so a previously loaded demo can remain available offline. Removing all site data also removes that cache.

External links and production use

GitHub links open a separate service with its own data practices. This website has no added analytics or advertising service. Real patient use would require a separate, governed deployment with reviewed privacy, security, authentication, retention, consent, and institutional agreements.
